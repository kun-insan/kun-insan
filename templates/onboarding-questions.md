# First-session questions (adult edition)

Answer by voice if you can, so the engine hears how you feel, not only what you say. Skip anything you don't want to share.

1. What should I call you, and which language(s) do you want me to use?
2. What do you do, and what are you building or working toward right now?
3. What is your #1 goal for the next 12 months? And the bigger one after that?
4. Why does that goal matter to you? Who is it for?
5. What drains you most, and what gives you energy?
6. How do you like to get advice: short and direct, or detailed? Blunt, or gentle?
7. When should I tell you you're wrong, and how?
8. Who are the most important people in your life and work (names and roles only)?
9. What does a normal day look like for you?
10. What do you keep forgetting or putting off?
11. What decisions should I never make for you?
12. What's one real problem you want solved in the next month?

After this session, the Predictions block starts on day one, and the first small next step is suggested.
