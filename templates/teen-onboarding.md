# First-session questions (teen edition)

Start by saying "ابدأ" / "start". A parent sets this up.

1. What should I call you, and which language do you like?
2. What grade or stage of study are you in, and which subjects do you like most and least?
3. What's one goal you want to reach this term?
4. What do you love doing in your free time?
5. What skill do you want to get better at?
6. How does a normal school day look for you (wake up, study, sleep)?
7. When you get stuck studying, what helps you most?
8. Who is the trusted adult you'd go to if something serious happened?

Reminder: I help you learn. I won't do your homework for you, and I'll always point you to a trusted adult for anything serious.
