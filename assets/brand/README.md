# كن إنسان — Kun Insan brand assets

## The mark

A human figure, head and shoulders, sits above an open arc. The figure is the person; the arc is the engine: it carries from below, it never closes around the human, and it never stands in front. Shoulders and arc lie on the same circle, so the two together complete one whole, one Earth, with the human at the top leading it.

The mark is deliberately plain: no religious symbol, no flag, no face, no gender, no skin tone.

## Colors

| Name  | Hex       | Use |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Ink   | `#13243A` | The arc, the wordmark, dark backgrounds |
| Amber | `#DE9526` | The human figure on light backgrounds |
| Amber (on dark) | `#EBAA45` | The human figure on Ink |
| Paper | `#F6F1E7` | The arc and wordmark on dark backgrounds; warm light background |

Amber is for the human figure only. Do not set text in it.

## Wordmark

Arabic: Noto Kufi Arabic SemiBold. English: Inter Medium, capitals, widely tracked to the width of the Arabic. Both are converted to outlines in the SVGs, so no fonts are needed to display them. Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License.

## Usage

Do:

- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the diameter of the head.
- Use `logo.svg` on white or Paper, `logo-dark.svg` on Ink or other dark backgrounds.
- Use the mono mark when only one color is available (print, stamps, embossing).
- Use the mark alone below about 120 px wide; the wordmark needs room.

Don't:

- Recolor, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows and gradients.
- Close the arc into a full ring, or move the figure inside it.
- Add faces, religious, national or political symbols to the mark.
- Place the two-color mark on mid-tone or busy backgrounds; use the mono mark there.
- Retype the wordmark in another font.
