# كن إنسان — Kun Insan brand assets

Official logo designed by **Amr Sharaf** (November 2026).

## The mark

فلسفة الشعار / Logo concept, in the designer's words:

1. **كلمة "كن"**: the mark is drawn from the letters of "كن", the first word of the project's name.
2. **شكل إنسان مرح بداخله طاقة وحيوية**: the same shape is a joyful human figure, full of energy and life, which stands for the second word, "إنسان".

One shape, both words: the name itself becomes a human.

The mark is deliberately plain: no religious symbol, no flag, no face, no gender, no skin tone.

## Usage

Do:

- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the diameter of the head.
- Use `logo.svg` on white or light backgrounds, `logo-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds.
- Use the mono mark when only one color is available (print, stamps, embossing).
- Use the mark alone below about 120 px wide; the wordmark needs room.

Don't:

- Recolor, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows.
- Separate or rearrange the parts of the mark.
- Add faces, religious, national or political symbols to the mark.
- Retype the wordmark in another font.
