# Contributing to كن إنسان

Thank you for wanting to build this with us. We grow the team step by step, based on trust and real contribution.

## Access levels

| Level | Who | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **Reader** | Anyone | This public repo: vision, architecture, roadmap. Open issues and discussions. |
| **Contributor** | Accepted applicants | Read access to the private engine repo. Contribute through pull requests, reviewed by core builders. |
| **Core builder** | Contributors with a proven track record | Write access, design decisions, review rights. |

Moving up is based on the quality of your contributions and how well you live the principles, not on time or titles.

## How to apply

Open an issue titled **`Apply: <your name>`** and tell us:

1. Who you are and what you've built (links: GitHub, LinkedIn, papers, products)
2. How you understand AI and where you add value
3. Why you want to build كن إنسان, and which principle matters most to you
4. A real problem in your life or work this should solve

We read every application. Shortlisted people get a short call with a real human from the team before access is granted.

## Ground rules

- **Sign your commits** with the Developer Certificate of Origin: `git commit -s`. It confirms you have the right to submit your work under the project license.
- **Never commit personal data**, credentials or real user content. Use synthetic examples only.
- Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Discuss big changes in an issue before writing code.

## Good first contributions

- Review [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) and open issues where it's unclear or could be better
- Propose memory block types and onboarding questions
- Privacy and security reviews
- Arabic, English and other language work
