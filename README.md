# كن إنسان — Kun Insan (Be Human)

> An AI that doesn't pretend to be human. It helps humans be better humans.

**كن إنسان** is an open AI engine you receive *empty*, with only guardrails. It learns from you (voice or text), adapts to you, and guides you to do things the right way, honestly and clearly, without noise.

It is **created, not a creator**. You direct it. It won't do everything for you, and it tells you plainly when something is right, when it's wrong, and when it can't help.

**Target: 8 billion people.** Any religion, any background, any country. It speaks from science and evidence, adapts to your views *the right way*, and never lies to you.

---

## Why open?

Because this can't be built by one person. The goal is helping humanity, not owning it. If you share the goal and the principles, build it with us. If you take the ideas and succeed with them, that still serves the mission.

## Principles

1. **Clarity and transparency.** No noise, no flattery, no hidden agendas.
2. **Says "I don't know."** When it lacks information it asks for exactly what it needs instead of guessing.
3. **Your data is yours.** Private by default. Built on standard legal frameworks and proven cloud security, not reinvented crypto.
4. **Earned abilities.** New capabilities unlock as the AI proves it's accurate for *you*, not just because it's full of data.
5. **Human in charge.** It advises and acts only within the permissions you grant.
6. **For everyone.** No discrimination by race, religion or belief. Guided by human rights.

## How it works (short)

```
You ──▶ Orchestrator ──▶ Memory blocks (empty slots that fill as it learns you)
            │                  │
            │                  └─▶ Abilities unlock by proven accuracy
            ├─▶ Auditor / Watcher (flags conflicts, wrong or unclear info)
            ├─▶ Skills (4 permission levels)
            └─▶ Guardrails (always on)
```
