# Playbook: getting attention by being human and honest

The founder used this strategy on his personal X account on 21 Nov 2026 and is sharing it openly, because Kun Insan's first rule is to be human and honest, and that includes how we grow.

## The strategy, step by step

### 1. Start from something you truly care about
Don't open with the product. Open with a real cause that hurts you personally. In this case it was the war in the founder's home country and what it does to ordinary people. The feeling has to be real, because people can tell when it isn't.

### 2. Write in your own voice, with emotion
- Write the way you speak, in your mother tongue and your own dialect, not in corporate language.
- Say who you are, what you believe in, and that you have no hidden interest, follow no one and fear no one.
- If faith is part of who you are, let it show naturally.
- Offer something concrete from yourself. Here the founder offered to act as a neutral, honest bridge between the sides.

### 3. Go straight to the highest-reach people
Instead of waiting for an audience to grow slowly, tag the most visible decision-makers on the topic directly, people with millions of followers. Their names in your post put it in front of their teams, their followers and the media who watch them.

### 4. Keep going for a short, intense window
Over about 12 hours (from the early morning to midday), the founder posted several messages and replies on the same topic, tagging the same high-reach accounts. Each message added one more piece rather than repeating the last.

### 5. Turn the attention to one thing: the work
The last message carried only two things: the link to the Kun Insan GitHub repo, and one short line saying this was his final message on the topic and that he had said what he needed to say. No pitch, no explanation. Anyone who followed the story ends up at the work.

### 6. Clean up right away
As soon as he felt he had their attention:
- He deleted every political post and every political reply, including the posts that tagged the high-reach accounts.
- He removed the pinned post on the topic.
- He kept his warm, personal replies to friends, because they show the human side and aren't political.
- His profile went from 248 posts to 171, and the Kun Insan link stayed as the latest post.

### 7. Tell the story openly
Last, he published this playbook in the public repo, so nothing is hidden. Anyone can see what was done and why.

## Why he believes it works
- Honest emotion travels further than polished marketing.
- Reaching the most visible people directly can get you seen in hours rather than months.
- Attention is only worth something if it lands on real work. That's why everything else gets removed and only the link stays.
- A short, intense window creates curiosity, and the clean-up leaves a clear, calm profile for anyone who comes looking.

## The risks, said honestly
- Tagging political leaders during a war can tie your name to one side, whatever you meant.
- Deleting posts doesn't erase them. Screenshots and notifications stay.
- People who took the messages as sincere may feel used once they read this page.
- Clients and partners in the region can see all of it.

He weighed these risks and decided to publish anyway. Kun Insan doesn't hide its own story.

## Rules if you use it
- Only say what you truly mean. If it isn't honest, don't post it.
- Don't attack or insult anyone, and don't speak for anyone without their permission.
- Never use another person's or company's name to back you up without their written OK.
- Point all attention at one real thing you built and tried yourself.
- Remove what's off-topic, but never lie about what you posted.

# دليل: كيف تلفت الانتباه وأنت إنسان صادق

استخدم المؤسس هذه الطريقة على حسابه الشخصي في X يوم 21 نوفمبر 2026، ونشرها بشكل مفتوح لأن أول قاعدة في كن إنسان هي الصدق، حتى في طريقة النمو.

## الطريقة خطوة بخطوة

1. **ابدأ من شي يهمك فعلاً.** لا تبدأ بالمنتج. ابدأ بقضية حقيقية توجعك. هنا كانت الحرب في بلد المؤسس وأثرها على الناس.
2. **اكتب بصوتك وبمشاعرك.** بلغتك ولهجتك، مش بلغة الشركات. قل من أنت وبإيش تؤمن، وإنك ما تتبع أحد ولا لك مصلحة ولا تخاف من أحد. وقدّم شي من نفسك: هنا عرض يكون جسر محايد وصادق بين الأطراف.
3. **روح مباشرة لأصحاب التأثير الأكبر.** بدل ما تنتظر جمهور يكبر ببطء، اعمل منشن لأكبر أصحاب القرار في الموضوع، ناس متابعينهم بالملايين.
4. **فترة قصيرة ومكثفة.** خلال حوالي 12 ساعة نشر عدة رسائل وردود على نفس الموضوع، وكل رسالة تضيف شي جديد.
5. **وجّه الانتباه لشي واحد: الشغل.** آخر رسالة فيها شيئين بس: رابط كن إنسان على GitHub، وسطر قصير إن هذه آخر رسالة منه في الموضوع وإنه قال اللي عنده.
6. **نظّف فوراً.** حذف كل البوستات والردود السياسية والبوست المثبّت، وخلّى ردوده الشخصية الطيبة مع أصحابه. الحساب نزل من 248 بوست إلى 171، ورابط كن إنسان صار آخر بوست.
7. **احكِ القصة بصراحة.** في الأخير نشر هذا الدليل في الريبو العام، حتى ما يكون في شي مخفي.

## المخاطر
المنشن لقادة سياسيين وقت الحرب ممكن يحسبك على طرف. والحذف ما يمسح الصور ولا الإشعارات. وبعض الناس ممكن يحسون إنهم انستخدموا لما يقرون هذا، والعملاء والشركاء في المنطقة يشوفون كل شي. قرر ينشر رغم هذا، لأن كن إنسان ما يخبي قصته.

## القواعد
قل بس اللي تعنيه. لا تهاجم أحد ولا تتكلم باسم أحد بدون إذنه. لا تستخدم اسم شخص أو شركة بدون موافقته المكتوبة. وجّه كل الانتباه لشي حقيقي بنيته وجربته بنفسك. ولا تكذب أبداً عن اللي نشرته.
