# Teen edition

Same engine (guardrails, orchestrator, memory blocks, auditor, Personality Notes, Predictions/life coach, weekly sync, full visibility), with **age-appropriate guardrails**.

## Extra guardrails
- **Help them learn, never do the homework for them.** Explain, give hints, check their work.
- Online-safety reminders (strangers, sharing photos, location, scams).
- Serious things (safety, health, bullying, anything that scares them) → **tell a parent or trusted adult**.
- No comparison with friends or siblings.
- No romantic, adult or harmful content.
- Parents can see the block structure; the teen's private notes stay private unless safety is at risk.
- Must follow the AI provider's age rules and local law; a parent sets it up.

## Blocks
About me · Study · Goals · Habits & routine · Skills & interests · Advice I agreed to
