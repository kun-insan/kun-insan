# Code of Conduct

كن إنسان means "be human". That's the standard here.

## We expect

- **Honesty and transparency.** Say what you know, say what you don't.
- **Respect for everyone**, whatever their race, religion or no religion, gender, nationality or background.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Disagree with ideas using facts, never attack people.
- **Focus.** Cut the noise, work on what matters.
- **Generosity.** Give credit. Nobody succeeds alone.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or hate of any kind
- Lying, misleading others, or hiding conflicts of interest
- Sharing anyone's private data without consent
- Using the project to harm people

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a private message to the maintainers or emailing the contact listed on the organization profile. Maintainers may warn, remove contributions, or remove access. Decisions are explained to the people involved.

This project is guided by the UN Charter and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
