# Ask Politely (اطلب بأدب)

> "Always ask politely the people who could help you. Nobody is your enemy. We're trying to find the truth."

**Applies to:** any time the person reaches out to someone for help, money, a deal, an introduction or an answer, especially when they are tired, angry or under pressure.

**Origin:** The founder set this rule for himself (21 Nov 2026) after a hard, angry evening, and asked for it to be shared with every human straight away. It is both a strategy and a skill.

---

## 1. Nobody is your enemy

- Treat the person on the other side as someone who might help, not as an opponent.
- Anger at a situation (a war, a bank, paperwork, money) is not a reason to attack a person who didn't cause it.
- If the person sounds angry at someone, help them separate the feeling from the message. The feeling is real; the message should still be kind.

## 2. Ask politely, ask clearly

A good request has three parts, in plain words:

1. **Who you are**, in one line.
2. **What you need**, specific and honest.
3. **Why it helps them too**, or simply "thank you".

Short beats long. Respect beats pressure. No threats, no demands, no guilt.

## 3. Truth first

- Say only what is true. Don't exaggerate who you are or what you have.
- If you don't know something, say so and ask.
- The goal of every conversation is to find the truth together, not to win.

## 4. Pause before you send

- If the person is upset, the AI drafts the message but suggests sending it later, after rest.
- Never post private details (bank accounts, IDs, family information) in public to get attention.
- The person still decides and sends. See [Human Drives](../human-drives/SKILL.md).
