# Clean My Footprint (نظّف أثري)

> Everyone makes mistakes. Some of them stay online. This skill helps a person fix theirs without losing who they are or what they are building.

**Max permission level:** Act with approval (level 3). Nothing is deleted without the person's yes on a shown list.

**Origin:** The founder used it first on his own personal X account (21 Nov 2026). He asked for it to be shared with every human who wants to fix a mistake and doesn't know how.

---

A person posted things they now regret, such as politics, anger, fear or noise. They want a clean, honest profile. Treat this as a human moment first and a technical task second.

## Unlock: milestones come before any deletion

The skill does nothing destructive until the person has reached all four milestones. Go through them in order, without skipping any.

1. **Heard.** Acknowledge the feeling in one or two sentences, without judging what they posted. If they sound unsafe or in crisis, stop here and support them as a person instead.
2. **Clear intent.** People's words are often mixed ("it was wrong" / "it was my right"). Name the conflict and offer plain choices:
   - (a) remove everything on the topic
   - (b) keep the core message and remove only the noise
   - (c) list it all and let them decide each item

   Recommend (c).
3. **Protected list.** Ask what must never be touched: their product, their work, their family. Treat family-related content, such as a relative's posts they reposted, as ask-first.
4. **Approved list.** First read the whole account without changing anything: posts, replies, reposts, following and bio. Show a grouped list (keep / delete / your call), with a recommendation on every "your call" item. Delete nothing until they say yes. "I trust you" counts as a yes to the list and recommendations you showed, and to nothing beyond them.

## Doing the clean-up

- Work in the person's own signed-in browser. Never type their password. If the right account isn't signed in, give them the exact clicks to sign in themselves.
- Delete one item at a time through the site's own menus (… → Delete → Confirm). Match each item by its exact post ID, never by its position on the page.
- Work in this order: original posts and threads, then replies, reposts, political or risky follows, and finally the bio.
- Log every action. Retry each failure once, then re-scan the profile for leftovers, because sites often show only part of the history at a time.
- Prefer reversible actions (undoing a repost, unfollowing) over permanent ones when either would do.

## Finishing

- Report the counts before and after, what was removed in each group, and what was kept and why.
- Flag anything that looked odd, such as a follow count that changed unexpectedly.
- Suggest a bio that points to what they are building.
- End warmly and briefly. Remind them their voice still matters, and they can say it again later, calmly and more strongly.

## Never

- Delete anything before milestone 4.
- Touch protected items, even when they mention a deleted topic.
- Lecture the person about what they posted.
- Store or repeat what they deleted beyond the session report.
