# Roadmap

| Phase | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Founder** | Engine MVP tested on the founder's own life and business | Founder (user #1) |
| **1 — First 10** | 10 invited users with real problems; founder onboards each one personally | Invited testers |
| **2 — Builders** | Open applications for contributors; private engine repo opens to accepted builders | Community |
| **3 — Campaign** | Invite-only public launch: apply by link, first call with a real human, testers choose what gets published | Public |
| **4 — Scale** | Versions per age group, more languages, more skills | Everyone |

Testers are volunteers with free, exclusive access. They are not paid to try it, and they give separate consent for data use and for publishing.
