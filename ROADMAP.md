# Roadmap

| Phase | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Founder** (now) | Engine tested on the founder's own life and business. Full design open-sourced. | Founder (user #1) |
| **1 — Builders** (now) | Anyone can pick a task from [tasks/](tasks/) and send a PR. Best builders join the core team. | Community |
| **2 — First 10** | 10 invited users with real problems; the founder onboards each one personally | Invited testers |
| **3 — Campaign** | Invite-only public launch: apply by link, first call with a real human, testers choose what gets published | Public |
| **4 — Scale** | Mobile app, versions per age group, more languages, more skills, published hardware guide | Everyone |

Testers are volunteers with free, exclusive access. They are not paid to try it, and they give separate consent for data use and for publishing.
